Performance & privacy comparison

When exporting animated video, many tools upload frames to a remote server queue. Product Shot encodes MP4 video directly on your GPU using WebCodecs at up to 120 FPS with zero server uploads, keeping your unannounced product screenshots completely confidential.

How does video export compare between Product Shot and Shots.so?

Product Shot supports 30, 60, and 120 FPS MP4 video exported client-side using WebCodecs. This produces smoother animation with instant rendering speeds.
