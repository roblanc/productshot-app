Product Shot for Mac

The same editor, in its own window: a Dock icon, no browser tabs, and your plan comes with you.

Download for Mac Free · Apple Silicon and Intel · macOS 12 or later

How do I install it?

  1. Open ProductShot.dmg from your Downloads.
  2. Drag Product Shot into the Applications folder.
  3. Open Product Shot from Applications or Launchpad.

macOS says it cannot verify the app. What do I do?

Product Shot is not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time you open it macOS asks you to confirm. You do this once:

  1. In the message that says Apple could not verify Product Shot, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
  3. Next to the line about Product Shot, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

After that it opens like any other app.

How do I sign in?

With your email, as on the web. In the app, type the code from the email: the link in the same email opens your browser, which is a separate app, so it signs in the browser rather than the app.

Does it need an internet connection?

Yes. The app opens the editor from getproductshot.com, so it is always the latest version and never needs updating. Image processing and exports still run on your Mac, as they do in the browser.

Is it different from the website?

No. It is the same editor with the same exports, and a Pro plan works in both. The Chrome extension sends its captures to Chrome, so use it from the browser.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Product Shot and drag it from Applications to the Bin.
