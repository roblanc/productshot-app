What is Product Shot?

Product Shot is a professional, local-first web application for creating beautiful screenshot and device mockups for App Store listings, marketing websites, pitch decks, and social media.

Which devices and frames does Product Shot support?

Product Shot includes iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, MacBook Air 13, MacBook Pro 13 and 15, unbranded laptops, iMac models and browser frames.

Can I export mockups with a transparent background?

Yes. Product Shot features a dedicated Transparent background preset and exports true transparent PNGs with full alpha channel preservation.

How much does Product Shot cost?

Design and preview are free, with clean 1× PNG exports and GIF exports carrying the Product Shot watermark. Pro costs $9 per month or $69 per year and adds PNG above 1×, GIF without the watermark, MP4, SVG, all batch exports, cloud composition sync.

How does the 1-Click Chrome Extension work?

Click the Product Shot icon in your Chrome toolbar or press Alt+Shift+S (or ⌥+⇧+S on Mac) on any tab. It captures the visible page, copies the PNG to your clipboard, and opens the mockup editor with your screenshot mounted.

Are my screenshots uploaded to your servers?

Image processing and exports run locally in your browser. For signed-in subscribers, saved compositions can sync automatically to their account, uploading the composition and its images to private cloud storage for access across devices. The local working copy stays on your device.

Which export formats are supported?

Product Shot exports PNG, GIF, MP4 and SVG. SVG preserves editable text, shapes and gradients while embedding screenshots, photographic hardware and pixel effects as raster layers. MP4 uses WebCodecs in supported browsers at 30, 60 or 120 FPS. 60 FPS is the default; 120 FPS is the heavier local maximum.
