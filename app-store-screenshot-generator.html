Good to know

A few practical questions.

What dimensions does Product Shot provide for App Store screenshots?

Product Shot provides presets for Apple's required 6.9″ display (1320×2868 px for iPhone 17 Pro Max / 16 Pro Max) and 6.7″ display (1290×2796 px), as well as standard iPad Pro dimensions.

Can I create animated preview videos for the App Store?

Yes. You can use motion presets to create smooth 30 or 60 FPS MP4 video previews matching Apple's App Preview video specifications.

Are my app screenshots kept private during creation?

Yes. Processing runs locally on your browser. Your unreleased app screens are never uploaded to external servers or exposed to public AI models.
