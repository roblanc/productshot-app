Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: Product Shot is local-first. Your edits, screenshots, and exports stay in your browser unless you explicitly save a composition to your account.

1. Overview

Product Shot ("we", "our", or "us") operates getproductshot.com and the Product Shot browser extension. This policy explains the data used to run the editor, accounts, subscriptions, support, and optional cloud sync.

2. Local editing

The normal editor works in your browser. Screenshots, uploaded images, mockup settings, and exports stay on your device while you work. We do not use advertising trackers and do not sell personal data.

3. Account and sign-in data

Accounts are created after a successful subscription checkout. We use your email address to identify your account and send passwordless sign-in links. Authentication and account data are processed by Supabase; sign-in emails are delivered through our configured email provider.

4. Billing data

Payments are processed by Stripe. Product Shot receives the billing status and the identifiers needed to activate your plan and open the billing portal. We do not receive or store your full card number.

5. Optional cloud sync

If you choose to save a composition to your account, we store its scene data and uploaded assets in private Supabase storage so it can be available on your other devices. Local browser storage remains the working copy. You can delete synced compositions from your account.

6. Support and technical data

When you contact support, we use the information you include to respond and resolve the issue. Our hosting and service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and security logs to deliver and protect the service.

7. Browser extension

The extension acts only when you trigger it. It captures the visible area of the active tab and passes the image to Product Shot for local editing. It does not read browsing history or record keystrokes.

8. Your choices

  • You can clear local browser storage to remove work kept on that device.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription through the Stripe billing portal.
  • You can request account help or deletion by emailing support.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@getproductshot.com.
