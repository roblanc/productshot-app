よくあるご質問

A few practical questions.

What screen resolution works best for MacBook mockups?

Any desktop screenshot works. For crisp results on Retina displays, screenshots between 1920×1080 and 3456×2234 (16:10 or 16:9 ratio) match the MacBook frame naturally. Auto fit handles other aspect ratios without distortion.

Can I export MacBook mockups with transparent backgrounds?

Yes. Select the Transparent preset in the background picker. Product Shot exports true alpha channel PNGs, allowing you to drop MacBook mockups cleanly over any website background.

Can I animate the MacBook camera or add motion?

Yes. Apply motion presets like Push in, Orbit, or Float. Pro accounts can export smooth 30, 60, or 120 FPS MP4 video rendered client-side with hardware acceleration.
