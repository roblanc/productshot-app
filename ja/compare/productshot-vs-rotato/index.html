A few practical questions.

Does Product Shot require a Mac to use?

No. Unlike Rotato which requires macOS, Product Shot works in Google Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and Arc on any operating system.

Can Product Shot create 3D camera motion like Rotato?

Product Shot offers curated 3D camera angles, matrix tilt transforms, and smooth motion presets (Orbit, Float, Push in) designed specifically for product announcements.

What formats can I export from Product Shot?

Product Shot exports clean PNG (with full alpha transparency), animated GIF, 30/60/120 FPS MP4 video, and vector SVG.
